Open your Timeline export and get a clear, private report: fuel spent, trips taken, places that mattered and how your days were really spent. Everything happens on this device — this page can’t even reach the internet.

Google now keeps Timeline on your phone, so the export comes from there:
  1. Android: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data.
  2. iPhone: Google Maps → your profile picture → Your Timeline → ⋯ → Location and privacy settings → Export Timeline data.
  3. Move the Timeline.json file to this computer and drop it above.
No accounts, no tracking, no AI. Open source.Your names and corrections are saved only in this browser.
